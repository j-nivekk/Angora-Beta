# Angora 1.1.2-beta.1

Thank you for testing Angora. Angora Beta 1.1.2 is a focused update that sharpens two of Angora's most-used tools — Quick Comment and Delete — and polishes the *glass* they sit on. 

## At a glance

- **Quick Comment becomes a tool in its own right** — a dedicated button, a visible armed state, and a flow that stays out of your way.
- **A more precise eraser** that trims exactly what you select and leaves the rest intact.
- **Cleaner Liquid Glass**, with the overlay toolbar's shimmer over busy pages put to rest.

## Quick Comment

Quick Comment lets you attach a comment to any existing annotation. In 1.1.2 it steps out of the shadows.

- **A button you can see.** Quick Comment now has its own control in both the standard toolbar and the Liquid Glass overlay, set just apart from the annotation tools. A soft, pulsing indicator shows at a glance when it is armed — no longer a shortcut you have to remember.
- **It holds its place.** Quick Comment now stays armed. Comment one annotation, then the next, then the next; it waits until you dismiss it, rather than standing down after every note.
- **One mode at a time.** Quick Comment and the annotation tools no longer compete for the cursor. Arming one quietly stands the other down — and the tool you were using, along with its colour, returns the moment you leave Quick Comment.

## A more precise eraser

The Delete tool has been reworked for the way text markup actually behaves.

- **It trims, it doesn't flatten.** Drag across part of a highlight, underline or strikethrough and only that span is removed. The remainder is preserved (comment and identity intact) and stitched back together.
- **Line-accurate.** Erasing on one line leaves tightly spaced neighbours untouched, even in dense titles and headings.
- **Improved Undo/Redo.** Each erase is its own step, and trimmed annotations survive later merges cleanly through undo and redo.

## Refinements

- **Cleaner glass.** The overlay toolbar's Liquid Glass background has been reworked to remove the occasional shimmer and banding that appeared over busy pages.
- **Broader localisation,** including a fully localised first-run onboarding.

## Updating

If you are already running Angora, choose **Check for Updates…** and Sparkle will offer 1.1.2 (Smoke Pearl). Otherwise, download the latest `.dmg` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/j-nivekk/Angora-Beta/releases), open it, and drag Angora into your Applications folder.

The Beta is signed with an Apple Development certificate but is **not notarised by Apple**, so macOS Gatekeeper will block it on first launch. You only need to do this once:

- **Recommended.** Control-click **Angora Beta** in Applications → **Open** → **Open**.
- Or, after a blocked attempt, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- Or, from Terminal: `xattr -cr /Applications/Angora\ Beta.app`.

## System requirements

- macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel
