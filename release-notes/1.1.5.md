# Angora 1.1.5-beta.1

A small release: printing, a fix to how the annotation list navigates, and the first experimental feature — marking up scanned pages.

## At a glance

- **File → Print… (⌘P)** — prints the document with your annotations on it.
- **Annotation list rows scroll to the annotation**, not to the top of its page.
- **Experimental: recognised text on scanned pages** — markup on a scan keeps its quote. Off until you turn it on.

## Printing

- **File → Print… (⌘P)** opens the standard macOS print dialogue.
- Annotations print as they appear on screen.
- Pages scale down to fit the paper and rotate to match its orientation, so an A4 document prints on US Letter and landscape pages come out landscape.
- Unavailable when no document is open, when the PDF is locked, or when its author disallowed printing.

## Annotation list

- Clicking a row scrolls the annotation itself into view instead of jumping to the top of its page, so on a dense page you no longer have to hunt for the markup you just selected.

## Experimental: scanned pages

Angora takes a highlight's quote from the PDF's embedded text. A scan or a photographed page has none, so markup on one has no quote — the sidebar shows an empty row, and search, copying and Markdown export have nothing to work with.

Switched on, Angora saves the wording macOS recognises under your markup alongside the annotation, so the quote survives closing and reopening the file.

- **Settings → Annotation → Experimental → Remember recognised text on scanned pages.** Off by default; turning it on asks you to confirm.
- **It writes into your PDFs.** The recognised wording is stored in the file in readable form — any app, including a plain text editor, can read every passage you marked up.
- **Overlapping markup stays separate** on these pages instead of merging, so it looks darker where it overlaps and appears as more than one row in the sidebar.
- **Trimming or resizing** markup on a scanned page can clear the wording saved for it.
- Unfinished, and may change or be withdrawn in a later version.

Pages that already carry embedded text are unaffected, whether the setting is on or off.

## Updating

If you are already running Angora, choose **Check for Updates…** and Sparkle will offer 1.1.5 (Smoke Pearl). Otherwise, download the latest `.dmg` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/j-nivekk/Angora-Beta/releases), open it, and drag Angora into your Applications folder.

The Beta is signed with an Apple Development certificate but is **not notarised by Apple**, so macOS Gatekeeper will block it on first launch. You only need to do this once:

- **Recommended.** Control-click **Angora Beta** in Applications → **Open** → **Open**.
- Or, after a blocked attempt, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- Or, from Terminal: `xattr -cr /Applications/Angora\ Beta.app`.

## System requirements

- macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel
